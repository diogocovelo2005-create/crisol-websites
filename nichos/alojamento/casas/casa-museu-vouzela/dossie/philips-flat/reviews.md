We stayed here for a week at the end of September 2017 for a holiday in the Sun. The apartment itself was excellent, even better than described, view was great, location ideal (quiet but only 5 minutes from both the beach and centre of Alvor), rooms and air-conditioning perfect, kitchen was a good size and the bathrooms were ideal.

Alvor proved to be a terrific wee town with an amazingly vibrant centre. Once we twigged that we should ignore the local restaurants and walk the 400m into town centre, this apartment's location became a perfect fit for us - away from the busy centre, away from the apartments directly over the pool. The view from balcony over the sea was excellent. Road was busier than predicted but we zoned that out very quickly. Pool was very good and we always got a lounger even when busy.
