**An absolutely wonderful, atmospheric place**

Gostou: It’s set in a historic house that was only recently restored, and the quality of the finishes in the rooms and common areas is excellent. The grounds are small but very picturesque; you can sip coffee with mountain views and pick figs straight from the tree.

The host is friendly and eager to help with even the little things — much appreciated, thank you. There’s a covered garage with an EV charger; it isn’t very powerful, but I was allowed to charge overnight for free.
The house itself is simply magical — though I think that’s clear from the photos. There’s even a tiny private chapel, especially handy if you’ve accumulated a few sins lately.

Another plus: several running and cycling routes start from this town in different directions. If you love running or biking, you’ll really enjoy the arched bridges and tunnels — as you go through them, you feel like an old steam locomotive from 1911.

Wishing the hotel every success — we’ll definitely be back, both to run and to relax.
